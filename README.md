# FINANACE-ANALYSIS-PROJECT
Power BI Finance Analysis project focused on financial transactions, customer segments, trends, and performance insights.

## Business Requirements
A financial organization wants to build an interactive Finance Analytics Dashboard in Power BI to monitor and analyze overall financial transactions, customer behavior, fees, taxes, and transaction performance across different business segments and regions.
**The management team faces challenges in tracking:**

•	Overall transaction growth and financial performance 

•	Monthly trends in transaction amounts 

•	Successful vs failed transactions 

•	Customer segment contribution 

•	State-wise financial performance 

•	Transaction type profitability 

•	Gender-based customer analysis 

•	Year-over-Year (YoY) performance changes 

**The objective of this dashboard is to provide a centralized analytical solution that helps stakeholders:**

•	Monitor KPIs in real time 

•	Identify high-performing customer segments and states 

•	Analyze transaction patterns and trends 

•	Track operational fees and taxes 

•	Understand customer demographics 

•	Improve financial decision-making and business strategy 

## KPI Requirements
## Main KPIs
**1. Total Amount**:	Displays the total transaction amount processed. Includes YoY growth comparison. 

**2.Total Transactions:**	Shows the total number of transactions performed. Tracks yearly transaction volume changes.
   
**3. Average Transaction Value:**	Calculates the average amount per transaction.
    
**4. Total Fees:**	Displays total fees collected from transactions.
    
**5. Total Tax:** Shows total tax generated from all transactions'

## Chart Requirements
**1. Total Amount by Month**
**Chart Type:** Line Chart / Area Chart

•	Success 
•	Failed 
•	Pending 

3. Total Amount by Customer Segment

4. **Total Amount by State**
**Chart Type:** Horizontal Bar Chart
**Objective:** Compare state-wise transaction amounts to identify top-performing regions.

**5. Transaction Type Analysis**
**Chart Type:** Matrix / Heatmap Table
**Objective:* Understand performance and profitability by transaction category.


6. Total Amount by Gender
**Chart Type:** Donut Chart
**Objective:** Analyze transaction amount contribution by:
•	Male

Helps understand customer demographic participation.

## Analytical Highlight
**1.Strong Transaction Value:** Generated ₦137.49M from approximately 15K transactions, with an average transaction value of ₦9.20K.

**2.Positive Revenue Growth:** Total amount increased by 1.43% YoY, while average transaction value grew 2.00%, indicating higher transaction value despite a 0.57% decline in transaction volume.

**3.Controlled Costs:** Total fees were ₦216.87K, while tax amounted to ₦39.04K, providing visibility into transaction-related costs.

**Below is an in-depth analysis of each visual component:**

 **1. total_amount by month (Line Chart):**  Revenue surges sharply during July and December but plummets heavily in February and September.
 
**Insight:** The business follows a strict seasonal cycle driven by summer and holiday spending. Strategic marketing campaigns must be launched during early spring and autumn slumps to smooth out cash flow gaps.

**2. total_amount by transaction_status (Donut Chart):** Success statuses overwhelmingly dominate the visual, while Failed and Pending metrics occupy minuscule slivers.

**Analyst Insight:** This proves exceptional payment gateway infrastructure stability. High processing success rates protect merchant trust, keep customer frustration low, and significantly lower operational overhead tied to refund reconciliations.

 **3. total_amount by customer_segment (Bar Chart)** : Retail and Premium segments tie for the top spot, while the high-net-worth Wealth segment trails significantly at the bottom.
 
 **Insight:** Revenue relies safely on a diversified mass-market base. However, the low Wealth volume highlights an unexploited sector; the business must introduce premium, high-value investment products to attract high-tier capital.

 **4. total_amount by state (Bar Chart)**: Maharashtra holds a massive, overwhelming revenue lead, completely outpacing all other trailing states like Karnataka.
 
**Insight:** The platform faces severe geographic concentration risk. Relying on a single economic hub leaves the business highly vulnerable to local regulatory shifts, disasters, or targeted competitor regional campaigns.

**5.Transaction type analysis (Data Table):** Loan EMI and Transfers generate the highest revenue volumes, while card and bill payments pull lower engagement.

 **Insight:** The platform serves as a critical credit-repayment and liquidity utility. Because loan repayments are repetitive, non-negotiable monthly actions, this feature drives highly predictable, long-term user retention.
 
**6.total_amount by gender (Donut Chart):** Males generate 52.79% of transaction volume, while Females closely trail at 47.21%.

**Insight:** The platform boasts a highly resilient, demographically balanced consumer base. This nearly equal gender split reduces market exposure risk and makes the application incredibly attractive for broad consumer partnerships.

## Core Business Recommendations

**Mitigate Regional Risk:** Expand aggressive marketing and merchant acquisition frameworks outside Maharashtra to diversify regional concentration and protect the core pipeline.

**Target Premium Wealth** Launch high-yield investment structures and wealth management features to capture the underutilized, high-capital Wealth segment market share.

**Flatten Seasonal Dips:** Implement targeted cash-back promotions and transaction discounts during historic February and September slumps to stabilize monthly cash flow.

**Leverage Core Features:** Bundle secondary services like bill payments with Loan EMI and Transfers to cross-sell and boost lower-performing utilities.

## Conclusion
The platform exhibits highly stable financial health driven by mass-market retail reliance, high transaction values, and strong infrastructure. Long-term scaling depends entirely on balancing regional concentration risks and capturing underperforming high-net-worth wealth segments.
